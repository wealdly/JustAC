## [Unreleased]

### New
- WoW Forever: JustAC can add the buffs and damage-over-time effects your rotation depends on to your Cooldown Manager, so it follows them exactly in combat. It asks once, the first time your rotation needs one that isn't tracked, and you can turn the question off. It only adds effects you have never moved yourself, puts them in the panel you use least, and can take back everything it added in one click (General > Blizzard UI). If your spec is on the default layout, it creates a layout named JustAC for it rather than touching the default; removing its additions deletes that layout again.
- WoW Forever: the swing bar on Auto Shot and wands works as a kiting helper: it fills orange while you can keep moving, turns green the moment the shot is ready - stand still and it fires right then - and starts again as the shot lands, from the moment you switch it on. Swing bars are slimmer too.
- WoW Forever: hunters inside Auto Shot's 8-yard minimum range are offered Attack instead of an Auto Shot that cannot fire.

### Fixed
- WoW Forever: Raptor Strike, Heroic Strike, Cleave and Maul no longer lead the queue while you're out of melee range.
- WoW Forever: hunters open with Serpent Sting, cast together with Auto Shot. A condition on an ability you haven't learned yet (Aimed Shot while levelling) no longer keeps another ability off the queue.
